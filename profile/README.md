## `01` · THE IDEA

### We're not allergic to work. We're allergic to unnecessary work.

**The Slacker Dev** is a small development team focused on building useful digital experiences and the systems behind them. We write clean code, reduce friction, and let automation handle the repetitive parts—so our energy goes into solving problems that matter.

> **Our definition of slacking:** spending a little more thought today to save a lot more work tomorrow.
